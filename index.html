Föhn …
Warm, dry downslope wind — felt on the side it heads toward (Δp Lugano−Zürich). South Föhn (≥ +4): pours into the northern valleys — hits Urnersee, Walensee, Thunersee, Sihlsee, Alpnachsee (strong wind, gusty). North Föhn (≤ −4): pours into Ticino — suppresses the Inverna/Breva at Lago Maggiore & Dervio. Ground guide at Altdorf: +4 ≈ 15-25 kn S, +6-8 ≈ 25-40 kn (expert).

Bise …
Cold, dry NE wind that does not cross the Alps — it flows alongside them on the northern side, channelled along the Swiss Plateau from the Bodensee down to Lake Geneva (Δp Geneva−Güttingen). Drives the northern lakes: Bodensee, Wollishofen (Lake Zürich), Zugersee, Greifensee, Pfäffikersee. Ground guide at Payerne: +4 ≈ 10-15 kn NE, +6 ≈ 15-20, +8 ≈ 20-25 (overpowered).

South-side thermal — Inverna / Breva
The S afternoon thermal on the southern lakes, building on sunny, weak-gradient days from ~midday to sunset. Drives Lago Maggiore (Inverna) and Lago di Como / Dervio (Breva). Killed by North Föhn (negative Δp Lugano−Zürich). The Garda Ora (Δp Bozen−Brescia) is a same-day proxy: positive = Ora on Garda → Inverna likely.

Check before you go

Why this matters. Open-Meteo is a global model — it misses lake-specific microclimate. Before committing to a drive, cross-check the dashboard against (a) the spot's live wind station, (b) the live webcam, and (c) the synoptic charts above. Each card's badge shows whether the spot is actually rideable right now from the live wind (green = good wind for wingfoiling), regardless of whether the model agrees; the small line underneath shows how the live reading compares to the forecast, so a wide gap flags that the model is off — trust the live station / webcam first.

How scores are computed. Each spot's score combines forecast wind speed (12:00-18:00 local), gusts, afternoon cloud cover, day-night ΔT, and the Föhn/Bise pressure differentials computed from Open-Meteo mean-sea-level (MSL) pressure at four reference cities (Lugano, Zürich, Geneva, Güttingen). The heuristic per spot mirrors the checklists in the planning doc.
